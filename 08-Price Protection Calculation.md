## Downside Protection
Upon reaching the deadline, your Price Protection claim is equal to the price difference between the price-at-deadline and your protection-start-price multiplied by the amount of tokens you are protecting.

## Upside Protection
The claim for Upside Protection is a bit different. Upon reaching the deadline, the price difference is divided by the price of the protected token at deadline. This is because you will receive your claim in the protected token by default.

### Calculation Example
- You bought Downside Protection on 2 WETH with a protection-start-price of 2,500 USDC
- The price of WETH at your deadline is down to 2,300 USDC (-8.00%)
- You will be able to claim 200 (2,500 - 2,300) * 2 = 400 USDC
